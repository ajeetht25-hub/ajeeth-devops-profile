About

How I work

I'm a Cloud DevOps Engineer based in India, currently at Wells Fargo, where I design and run the infrastructure that sits underneath everyday deployments — CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes clusters, and the Terraform modules that provision them.

My background started in IT support before moving into DevOps, so I tend to think about infrastructure the way a support engineer does: what breaks, why, and how do we stop it from breaking again. That shows up as monitoring built in from day one (Prometheus, Grafana, CloudWatch), root-cause analysis instead of quick patches, and automation that removes the repetitive parts of the job — Terraform cut manual provisioning effort on my current team by up to 90%.

Outside pure infra work, I've trained teammates on new tooling and led escalation resolution across Dev, QA and Prod environments — the "make the team faster, not just the pipeline" side of the role.
